and SVG Logos assets distributed under CC0, and official brand assets used under
their published brand guidance. No separate license file is bundled.
These source records are for maintenance, not a required attribution notice.

Manus is an official brand asset, not a CC0 file. Its brand page provides this
icon for marketplaces and other compact representations of Manus. Keep the
original colors and geometry; follow its restrictions on rotation, flipping,
outlines and recoloring. The SVG is bundled unchanged.

Claude and Cursor use only the symbol from the full logo. Grok uses the symbol
without its app background, rendered in black. Adobe, Meta, Perplexity, Bilibili,
GCash, GoPay, Surfshark and 1Password display the symbol from the full logo.
View boxes are fitted for icon use. Tencent uses the PNG embedded in its source
SVG; Canva and Xiaohongshu also use local raster images.
Kiro's white symbol and black eyes are inverted for the light catalog background.

When adding assets, inspect both the file status and its original source. Do not
use files with disputed licensing, copied restrictive assets, or imitation marks.

Public-domain status can vary by jurisdiction. Brand names and marks remain
subject to their owners' trademark rights; these source statuses do not grant
trademark permission.
